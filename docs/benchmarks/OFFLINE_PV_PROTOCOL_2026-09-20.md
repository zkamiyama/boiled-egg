H2: at least some known stationary components benefit in independently measured amplitude/undesired-energy/pumping diagnostics without unacceptable pitch or transient regressions. Reject promotion if the candidate loses known pitch (5cents), creates nonfinite/empty/silent output, breaks exact length, or worsens absolute event-position error by >1ms or 5–95% energy width by >20% versus its paired iteration0.
